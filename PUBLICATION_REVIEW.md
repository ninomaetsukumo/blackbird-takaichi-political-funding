# JAPAN — JP-02 — Publication and Privacy Review

**Review date:** 27 September 2026  
**Result:** PASS — publication-ready. The standing unresolved-items register remains part of the published evidentiary boundary.

## 1. Evidence review

- Primary political-funds records are distinguished from secondary reporting.
- Complaints and allegations are labelled as allegations; no complaint is treated as guilt, indictment, or conviction.
- Filing corrections and refunds are described as record/accounting events, not evidence of intent or concealment.
- Public-program participation is separated from any claim that a political actor personally selected or directed the benefit.
- Shared addresses, officers, vendors, and same-date donations are treated as network evidence only; they are not converted into misconduct findings.
- Negative findings are phrased as failure to establish a relationship in defined public-source searches, not proof that no private relationship exists.

## 2. Political-neutrality review

- The report does not endorse, oppose, rank, score, or recommend any political actor, party, or electoral choice.
- It does not predict electoral outcomes or assess electability.
- Contested legal and political interpretations are attributed to identified complainants, media reports, or public records.
- Descriptive findings are separated from analytical judgment.

## 3. Privacy review

Removed or excluded from the public edition:

- unrelated private individuals;
- private family information;
- personal contact details;
- private residential addresses;
- weak social-media proximity;
- common-name identity matches without corroboration;
- private financial information not contained in lawful public records.

Public political-office addresses and publicly disclosed corporate/organisation identities are used only where necessary to explain the documented network.

## 4. Visual review

The English and Japanese reports include evidence visuals for:

1. overall case architecture;
2. donor-side relationships;
3. Branch B transaction sequence;
4. Ubusuna / Local 10,000 public-benefit chain;
5. Sōkōkai / Kameoka / Veanas / PoliLab / shared-address branch.

Visuals use solid lines for primary-record relationships where stated and dashed lines for reported/corroborated relationships that retain a primary-source upgrade requirement. A diagram is a relationship summary, not an allegation of misconduct.

## 5. Known unresolved items preserved

The public edition preserves, rather than hides, the principal gaps:

- original/intermediate versions of the corrected 2024 branch filing;
- original-versus-amended Nara Soshikai reports and underlying receipts;
- direct commercial-register extracts for Veanas and the Nakayama-linked companies;
- exact PoliLab legal entity and primary Shin-Jidai expenditure row;

- primary records for reported Kamunagara → World Heritage in-kind property transfers;
- provisional donor rows such as Nitori Holdings pending direct primary-line confirmation;
- future prosecutorial, regulatory, or judicial dispositions.

## 6. Closure decision

Broad discovery is closed. Reopen only if a new primary record, legal disposition, or genuinely new evidence materially changes an established finding or network edge.

**Core publication rule:** source ≠ claim ≠ fact ≠ conclusion.
